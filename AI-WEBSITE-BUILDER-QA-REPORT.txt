Fixes implemented
- Rebuilt the contact section that previously produced oversized orange editable boxes and a cramped CTA.

- Section controls now use a compact “Section” menu instead of a long toolbar covering content.

- Improved local fallback copy so visible website content is customer-facing rather than builder/instructional copy.
- Enhanced custom categories such as fashion, wellness and travel with more relevant fallback products/services and sample prices.
- Preserved inline text editing, image replacement, prices, section/page editing, colors, typography, spacing, undo/redo and reset.
