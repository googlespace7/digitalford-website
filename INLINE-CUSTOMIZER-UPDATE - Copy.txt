Digitalford Templates - Inline Live Customizer Update

Implemented:
- Text editing now happens directly beside the text inside the template preview.
- Editable text shows a pencil control in live edit mode; double-clicking text also starts editing.
- Changes sync immediately to repeated instances of the same field in the preview.
- Every template preview image receives a Replace image control directly on the image.
- Hero/background image replacement is available directly on background-image hero sections.
- Logo replacement is available beside the logo itself.
- Primary and accent colors are editable from the floating in-preview Live edit toolbar.
- Reset, Choose Template and Done are available in the same floating toolbar.
- Separate long form customizer fields are no longer required during live editing.
- Uploaded preview images are retained while the visitor stays on the Templates page session.
- Existing ratings, device previews, fullscreen preview, WhatsApp and Choose Template flows are preserved.

Image upload validation: JPG/PNG/WebP, maximum 2 MB per image.
