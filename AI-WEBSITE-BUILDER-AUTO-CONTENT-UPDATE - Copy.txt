DIGITALFORD AI WEBSITE BUILDER — AUTOMATIC CONTENT GENERATION UPDATE

Implemented changes
- Removed the separate Products & Services onboarding step.
- Added Business Type on the basic business-details step:
  Products + Services / Products / Services / Portfolio / Information.
- Business description now explicitly asks the visitor to mention the types of products sold and services offered.
- Added "Create Website Now" on Step 1 so a visitor can generate immediately after entering the required business basics.
- Audience, goals, style, colors and uploaded logo remain optional preferences.
- Reduced onboarding from 5 steps to 4.

Automatic website draft
- The generator now infers relevant products and/or services from the business description and category.
- It creates editable product/service names, descriptions and sample/demo price suggestions.
- It creates separate Products and Services sections where appropriate.
- It matches realistic category-appropriate Pexels sample photography for hero, product/service and supporting cards.
- It creates a starter SVG logo automatically when no logo is uploaded.
- The generated logo, all images, prices, names, copy, colors, pages and sections remain editable in the existing visual editor.

AI backend
- The secure PHP endpoint now accepts businessType and no longer requires manually entered offers.
- When OPENAI_API_KEY is configured, the AI is instructed to infer products/services and return editable sample pricing plus image keywords.
- The frontend applies realistic category-matched imagery to generated items.
- If the AI endpoint is unavailable, the existing smart local fallback still builds a complete editable draft using category knowledge plus terms extracted from the user's business description.

Pricing note
- Auto-generated prices are explicitly treated as editable sample/demo suggestions. They should be reviewed before publishing and are not presented as verified prices supplied by the business.

Images
- Default generated draft imagery is realistic category-matched Pexels photography loaded from the web.
- Images can be replaced directly inside the visual editor using the existing Replace Image controls.
- Internet access is required to display the default Pexels sample photographs.

No unrelated Digitalford pages were redesigned for this update.
